Delete your account

You can delete your account in the app: open Account, then Privacy and data, then Delete my account. If you cannot open the app, for example because you uninstalled it, you can do the same here.

What happens

  • Your account is switched off at once and you are signed out everywhere else.
  • You have 30 days to change your mind. Come back here or sign in to the app and choose Keep my account.
  • After 30 days your name, phone number, saved addresses, photos and notices are erased, and your phone number can be used to sign up again.
  • Orders and payment records stay, without your name or address, for as long as the law needs. Messages you wrote stay for the other person, shown as “Deleted user”, until their retention period ends.
  • We cannot delete an account while an order, a report or return, a store you own, or a payout or refund is still open. We will tell you what to finish first.

To download your data first, use Download my data in the app, under the same page.
