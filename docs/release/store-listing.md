## Full description

Walk into an Abuja market from your phone.

`{{APP_NAME}}` lets you look down on the whole market, fly into a store and browse its shelves, or just search and shop from a simple list. Every seller is identity-checked before they can sell.

**Pay safely.** Your payment is held until your order arrives. You confirm, then the seller is paid. If something is wrong, you can report it and we step in.

**Delivery or collection.** Choose delivery by courier or by the seller, or collect from a branch near you. Track every order and give the seller your handover code only when the goods are in your hands.

**Talk to the seller.** Ask about an item or an order inside the app. Messages are checked so everyone stays polite, and contact details and off-app payments are not allowed.

**Sell too.** Become a seller with an access code, open a store with branches and a team, add products with photos from the built-in camera guide, and manage orders from your phone.

**You are in control of your data.** Download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, from inside the app.

Available in Abuja first. Phones outside Abuja are told we are coming soon.
