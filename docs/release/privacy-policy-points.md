# What the privacy policy must say, draft

**Draft points for the owner, the DPO and a lawyer to turn into the real policy and the in-app notice. Not legal advice. Nigeria's NDPA 2023 is the primary law (NDPR also applies).** The policy URL goes in the server's `APP_PRIVACY_URL`; both stores require it before release.

## Who we are
- The controller's name and address, a contact for privacy questions, and the supervisory authority a person can complain to (the Nigeria Data Protection Commission).

## What we collect and why
- Account: display name, phone number, password (stored hashed).
- Delivery: saved addresses (name, phone, address line, landmark, area, postcode).
- Orders, payments (through a payment provider; we never see card details), disputes and reviews.
- Messages between buyer and seller, and the photos people upload.
- Sellers: store details, a payout account, and an identity check (NIN and a selfie, through an identity provider; we keep only a keyed fingerprint of the NIN, the verified name and the year of birth).
- A push token, to send notices.
- Approximate location is read on the phone to say whether we deliver there, and is not sent to us.
- We do not use advertising or analytics, and we do not sell data.

## Who else sees it (processors and recipients)
- The payment provider, the courier (name, phone, address for delivery), the SMS provider (phone, to send codes), an object store for photos, Google (Firebase) for push, the identity provider (sellers), and **an AI provider that checks chat text for tone** (text only; no names or ids).
- The seller sees the buyer's delivery details only where needed to deliver; a store's team may answer buyers, and members' names show to the team only.
- Any transfer outside Nigeria (the AI provider, Firebase, possibly the object store and the others) and its basis.

## How long we keep it (placeholders to confirm; the Owner can change the windows)
- Chat: 12 months, longer while a dispute is open. Delivery addresses: 12 months after an order ends. Notices: 6 months. Attempt records: 30 days.
- Order, payment and dispute records: 6 years, without your name, phone or address after you delete your account. **6 years is a placeholder that legal must confirm.**
- Chat messages you wrote stay for the other person, shown as "Deleted user", until the chat window ends.

## Your rights and how to use them
- Download your data and delete your account in the app (Account, Privacy and data). Deletion has a 30-day cooling-off and is refused while an order, dispute, store or payout is still open.
- **A web page or email address for people who cannot open the app** (the stores require it).
- Correct your details, object to or restrict processing, and complain to the regulator.

## Other
- Automated checks: chat messages are checked automatically; a person can review flagged ones.
- Children: the app is for adults.
- Breach notification: the 72-hour procedure is an owner action and is not written yet.
- Changes to the policy, and the date it was last updated.
