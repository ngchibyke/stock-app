# Part 7e-4: Chat in the App Implementation Plan

**Goal:** the owner-approved design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-07e4-chat-app-design.md`, approved 2026-11-26): the store's team answers buyers, and buyers and sellers chat in the app with every moderation outcome shown, live over the WebSocket with a polling fallback.

**Architecture:** Server first: `chat::service::access`, listing and open use one predicate (`seller_side_sql`) for the buyer or the store's side (owner, managers, store-wide staff, branch staff for their branch's order chats and for product chats); `Access.team` carries who live frames and notices go to; `MessageView.by` is told to the team only; managers and staff send only after the identity check (`identity_required`); unread counts the other side's messages against one shared read marker per side. App: `app/lib/features/chat/` with hand-parsed models, `ChatRepository` (API and fake), a `ChatSocket` interface (real one over `web_socket_channel`, fake for tests), a per-conversation controller that merges history, frames and the sender's own pending items, screens, and `ChatStrings`.

## Global constraints

- The server's filter is the authority: the app reports outcomes, never decides one. A retry reuses the same `client_message_id`.
- No transcript on disk, no message text in logs, routes, analytics or push. The socket carries the bearer header, never a token in the URL; one socket, open only while the app is in front.
- Buyers see the store's name and never which member wrote. Members see buyer labels, never a phone.
- Server: Postgres 17 suite from `server/`, fmt, clippy `-D warnings`. App: `build_runner`, `dart format`, `flutter analyze`, `flutter test`. Never claimed run on a device. No AI attribution in commits.

## Tasks

1. Server (built and tested in `tests/chat_team.rs`): access by role and branch, gate, notices, shared read marker, `by`, frames, open rules.
2. App models, `ChatRepository`, `ChatSocket`, strings; `ApiError.mutedUntil`.
3. The conversation controller (merge by `seq`, duplicates, gaps, pending, retry).
4. Conversation screen and every moderation state; composer with the cap.
5. Messages list; buyer entry points (product page, order page, Account); push and notice routing by `conversation_id`.
6. Seller side: Chats tab in the Sell area, role-aware, identity banner.
7. Wire check (REST and the socket) against a real local server; README, roadmap and plan status; full checks.
