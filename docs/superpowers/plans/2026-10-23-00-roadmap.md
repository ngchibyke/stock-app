# Marketplace Build Roadmap

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-abuja-marketplace-design.md`

The spec covers several independent subsystems, so it is built as a sequence of plans. Each plan ends in working, tested software. Only the plan marked **written** exists so far; each later plan is written once the one before it lands, so it can build on real interfaces instead of guessed ones.

| # | Plan | Delivers | Depends on | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Server foundation** | Rust API: sign-up/sign-in with phone OTP, sessions, roles, access codes, stores (max 2), products and variants, audit log, encrypted personal fields | — | **built** (`2026-10-23-01-server-foundation.md`, 92 tests) |
| 2 | Payments & escrow | Payment-provider layer with Mono (DirectPay + Disburse) first, seller payout accounts, cart, one payment split into orders per store, append-only ledger, 5% cut, release (handover code / 3 days after delivered), 30-day backstop queue, cancellations, 48h ship deadline, refunds, owner payment settings | 1 | **built** (`2026-10-23-02-payments-escrow.md`) |

## Carried forward from plan 1 (from its reviews)

**Plan 2 must start with these:**
- `PUT /products/{id}/variants` deletes and re-inserts variants (new ids, stock reset). Once orders reference variants, change it to upsert by attributes, or forbid replacing a variant that is referenced.
- No upper bounds on price, stock or weight yet. Add caps, and use checked arithmetic for kobo totals.
- Switch the three `users ... FOR UPDATE` locks (OTP issue, code redeem, store create) to `FOR NO KEY UPDATE` together. Global lock order: users → stores → products → variants → access_codes.
- Add graceful shutdown before payment webhooks arrive.
- Path ids: never use raw `Path<Uuid>`; use `Path<String>` + `parse_id`. Bodies use `AppJson<T>`.

**Before any public launch or real SMS provider:**
- Per-IP and per-phone limits on sign-up and sign-in (reverse proxy or app). Without them, anyone can lock a phone's sign-in for an hour, or keep a pending sign-up from verifying.
- The production database role must not own `audit_log`; revoke UPDATE/DELETE/TRUNCATE from the app role.
- DPO notes: sign-up's 409 `phone_taken` reveals whether a number has an account; failed-attempt tables keyed by user/phone need retention.

**Later plans:**
- Plan 8 (erasure): purge must clear or rotate `users.phone_idx` so a deleted number can sign up again; purge `redeem_attempts`, `sign_in_attempts`, old sessions and OTPs; purge `orders.delivery_address_enc` (setting `delivery_address_purged_at`) once the DPO has set its window.
- Upload plan: check that each photo key belongs to the uploading store.
- SMS-provider plan: a throttled resend can return a `verification_id` whose code was never sent; tell the client to wait and retry.
- Smaller polish items (input trimming, pagination and N+1 on store product lists, PATCH can't clear optional fields, empty PATCH writes an audit row).

## Carried forward from plan 2 (from its reviews)

**Plan 5 (disputes):**
- Clawback after release. Today a provider flag blocks auto-release and payout, but not a buyer-confirmed release.
- A refund in `processing` has no exit path. This has no impact while Mono refunds are not supported.

**Plan 6 (dashboard and reconciliation):**
- Alerts for late provider outcomes and payout amount mismatches are read from `audit_log` over a 30-day window, with no acknowledgement. Add acknowledgement, so that an unread double-payment alert cannot drop off the list.
- Re-poll successful payouts for bank reversals. Today a reversal is caught only if a webhook arrives.
- A daily statement reconciliation against Mono.
- Optionally, enforce posting keys and per-order balance in the database, beyond the existing zero-sum trigger.

**Before go-live (owner, legal, DPO):**
- Mono questions:
  - the amount units for DirectPay and Disburse;
  - whether lookup by `?reference=` lists in-flight disbursements;
  - what a duplicate payout reference returns;
  - the refund API;
  - the key prefixes;
  - whether repeated webhook failures disable an endpoint.
  
  The full list is in the plan-2 research notes.
- The CBN escrow arrangement, and the Mono/Flutterwave processor agreement and data location.
- Retention periods for payout account snapshots, `payout_attempts`, `provider_events`, refunds and audit rows.
- Whether sellers and the Owner should see the buyer's display name.
- Owner decisions:
  - "Received" before delivery;
  - payouts to suspended stores;
  - the notice period for fee changes;
  - immediate refund of a late payment on a flagged checkout.
- Four-eyes approval, step-up auth and rate limits for owner money overrides.

## Carried forward from plan 3 (from its reviews)

**Plan 5 (disputes and notifications):**
- Push notifications and reminders for delivery steps and the handover code.

**Plan 6 (dashboard and reconciliation):**
- Owner re-book for shipments in `booking_failed` or `booking_unknown`, and re-quoting expired courier quotes. No owner action clears these (or `cancel_failed`) today; the attention lists are the only net.
- Clearing sticky attention rows (they have no acknowledgement and sort oldest first).
- Repeated handover-lockout alerts: five wrong codes lock entry for an hour, but repeated lockouts raise nothing and there is no lifetime attempt cap.

**Later, unscheduled:**
- The NIPOST postcode adapter (only the manual provider exists).
- Courier `returned` and failed-attempt statuses (not yet known from Shipbubble).
- The Terminal Africa fallback aggregator.
- A map while out for delivery, only if the courier provides location.
- Purging soft-deleted delivery addresses (plan 8 purge list).
- Purging `orders.delivery_address_enc` after the DPO's retention window (plan 8 purge list): set `delivery_address_purged_at` in the same update, which is what lets the schema's check accept the NULL.
- Reconciling `booking_unknown` from the `shipment.label.created` webhook, if its payload proves to carry a matchable reference.
- The database pool `acquire_timeout` (30s) is longer than the 15s webhook budget; a slow acquire ends in a courier retry.

**Before go-live:**
- A re-book path should precede courier go-live: an empty Shipbubble wallet can fail up to 20 ready orders per tick for good, leaving them `paid` with the buyer's money held.
- Shipbubble sandbox smoke test: the amount unit, the webhook signing key and the exact bytes signed, address validation, the status field on `shipment.cancelled`, whether a used `request_token` can book again, what a 429 means, and what an insufficient wallet returns (it may look like any other 400).
- Gate on courier go-live, in the same sandbox: book a token whose `pickup_date` passed 1 and 2 days ago. The seller has 48 hours to mark ready and booking replays the quote's token, so a past date is the normal case. If it is refused, either quote again at booking (the difference absorbed and flagged through the existing tolerance) or quote for the end of the ready window, which changes the promise logic. Courier stays off for real stores until this is verified and the re-book path exists.
- Gate on courier go-live, in the same sandbox: does a cancelled label credit the wallet? Courier charges post to `courier_costs` at booking; no credit is posted on a cancel until this is known. If the wallet is credited, post the reverse under `("courier_refund", shipment_id)`, and have the accountant confirm.
- Set `SHIPBUBBLE_CONTACT_EMAIL` (platform operations address, sent when a contact has no email).
- Accountant: is delivery vatable at 7.5%, and who is merchant of record for courier delivery.
- DPO and legal review (items for review, not conclusions):
  - the Shipbubble processor agreement and data location;
  - a privacy notice that names couriers;
  - the seller seeing the buyer's address while the order is out for delivery;
  - retention of `orders.delivery_address_enc` (the buyer's name, phone, address and landmark, kept today on every order, including never-paid and refunded ones; a window has to be decided, and a cancelled order cannot be cleared before the 48h reverify window closes, because a late payment revives it), `courier_phone_enc`, `delivery_events`, soft-deleted addresses and audit rows;
  - the old business phone that can appear on labels for orders quoted before a phone change (T9d);
  - the buyer's name, phone and address go to Shipbubble when options are requested, before the buyer chooses courier or pays, including buyers who then pick self-delivery: earlier than "for the label", and perhaps earlier than the privacy notice says;
  - `buyer_addresses.postcode` is plaintext (ruling T2a covers the area council only), and an 11-character NIPOST code may locate a single property;
  - tracking URLs are plaintext and may be bearer links showing the address.
- Owner decisions:
  - whether a handover code should release after a delay, or have a no-code fallback, since a coerced code releases immediately;
  - whether the 48-hour ship deadline should pause on holidays and Sundays (today it is calendar hours).
- After deploying migration 0030, check `orders_attention` (paid orders with no delivery method, which the ship-deadline job never touches) and `shipments_attention`.

- Open a Mono business account with DirectPay and Disburse enabled (Disburse is live-only; it needs a source bank account approved by mandate). Ask Mono to confirm the amount unit (kobo or naira) and whether they offer bank-account name lookup and refunds by API. Needed by plan 2.
- Open Paystack (later, second checkout option) and Shipbubble business accounts. Shipbubble is needed by plan 3.
- Get legal/compliance review of the escrow setup (CBN) and DPO review of AI chat processing and retention. Needed before launch.
